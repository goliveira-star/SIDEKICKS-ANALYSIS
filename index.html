The headline

The program is not short of people. It is short of people who show up. 8 of 25 members produce 94.7% of all output, and 6 of 9 channels depend on a single active human, 5 of them with one person holding 97%+ of the volume. Meanwhile 12 members have either gone dark for 30+ days or logged fewer than 5 reactions in over two months.

Fixing German, Russian, Polish and Japanese matters more than adding headcount anywhere else.

Slowest 10% The 90th percentile. One report in ten waited at least this long. It is your worst realistic case, not your typical one.

Why the slowest 10% is the column to watch

German looks survivable at a 20.0h typical wait. But its slowest 10% is 77.1h, meaning one German report in ten sat unactioned for more than three days. Russian is 12.5h typical against 47.1h, Japanese 12.0h against 37.4h. For hate, doxxing and child-endangerment reports the damage doesn't scale evenly with delay, it compounds, so the tail is where the real exposure sits. Turkish (1.0h → 12.2h) and Spanish (1.4h → 7.6h) are the only two channels where the tail stays contained.

What this means

Four people who each single-handedly carry an entire language do not hold the role: annanow. (345 reactions, 100% of Polish), dusanr4d (159, French lead), nikitaalks (123, 100% of Russian) and sanela81 (97, 100% of German). Meanwhile three role holders have never reacted once.

So either the role isn't how sidekick access is actually granted, or assignment has drifted badly out of sync with who does the work. Resolve that before using role membership as the basis for access, perks, or payment. Right now it would reward three people who have done nothing and miss four who are load-bearing.

Typical wait is that member's median response time in hours; average wait is their mean, which a few very slow cases can inflate. "Per day" is contributions per active day, which separates someone genuinely steady from someone who did everything in one burst.

Decision rules applied

  1. Terminate: zero contributions ever, or dormant 30+ days with fewer than 50 contributions.
  2. Investigate: meaningful history but gone dark 30+ days, quiet 14–29 days, or currently active with fewer than 20 contributions.
  3. Maintain: active within 14 days and 20+ contributions.

Terminating all 10 costs you 1.0% of output and frees 10 seats. Before acting, confirm none of them were onboarded in the last few weeks. A new joiner and a ghost look identical in a 67-day window.
